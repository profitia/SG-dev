## 1. Scope and existing ownership

This amendment resolves the presentation-only ADP mounting decision left open by the [Platform AppShell Canon v1.0](v1.0-platform-appshell-business-and-architecture-canon.md), sections 5, 8, 11–14. It supplements [SRM Architecture & Component Placement v1.0](v1.0-srm-architecture-and-component-placement-canon.md), sections 3–4 and 16. It does not reopen their business, identity, tenant, provider, data or runtime boundaries.

ADP is a supplier-management capability of **SRM**, including SRM-CAP-001 and SRM-CAP-005. The supplier workspace may present the other related SRM capability areas without implementing their services. It is not a new business product or a new authentication/runtime authority. Architecture Platform retains the shared rules of Platform AppShell; **SG Runtime / SG2** owns its present physical host. CIC, Identity, X-Ray, Benchmark Finder and all product databases retain their existing ownership.

A native UI update requires a new SG2 host artifact; the SRM runtime itself is not deployed by this slice.

## 3. Presentation contract v1.0.0

The producer MUST publish one public mount and its types through the package export. No consumer imports its private implementation files. The contract records:

- logical entry `adp`, display name **ADP — Arkusz Danych Podstawowych**, SRM ownership, package/contract version and accepted source revision;
- locale `pl` or `en`, host-selected light/dark theme and a bounded token surface; the component does not own `documentElement`, global CSS, browser storage or the host router;
- a typed supplier-workspace presentation model, form values and explicit local UI events, with safe empty values and long/zero/multiline handling;
- local navigation state and host callbacks for route synchronization, return and dirty-state changes; no arbitrary external URL navigation;
- mount/reset/unmount semantics, listener/portal cleanup, focus and error handling, compatible version validation and rejection of unsupported contracts;
- **no Screen Context, CIC gateway, provider transport, credentials, account mutation, product persistence or authorization gateway in this iteration**.

The public mount renders host-supplied presentation data. The SG2 adapter owns the synthetic fixture and bounded local reactions. A source handler classified `symulacja` is excluded, not copied as product logic; an approved control can be backed by a separate local adapter under the user's explicit clickable-mockup scope. Server operations without a lawful local flow remain disabled. No synthetic server-success, sent mail, signed document, supplier verification or completed evaluation workflow may be claimed.

Product qualification is separate for **UI, content/data and behavior**, and is recorded against the pinned MagicPath revision outside the client import graph. Parent labels do not implicitly approve autonomous children. Developer inspectors, delivery registries, hidden scenario tools, source notes and service fixtures remain absent from product chunks, not merely hidden by CSS. The component can retain approved user-facing guidance and validation.

These are routes within the **existing SG2 host**, not new apps or services. The Module Host owns browser Back/Forward, deep links, refresh and navigation to the desktop. An unsaved in-memory draft MUST NOT be silently discarded by a host-controlled transition; the host preserves it for the mounted session or follows the approved discard/cancel flow. Hard refresh is not durable persistence. No new autosave or database rule is implied.

## 5. Explicit demonstration boundary

This release is **PRESENTATION_ONLY / LOCAL_STATE**. It may expose its synthetic workspace to users admitted by the existing AppShell account/access gate; it does not grant access to a private supplier capability, organization, supplier record or operation. There are no real supplier records behind the mockup. Do not invent a PORR organization ID, membership or ADP grant.

The package/adapter cannot call MGBI, Vercly, KRS, SAP, SRM/SG2 business APIs, maps, databases or CIC. The existing shared identity/session reads remain a host concern; their necessary authentication traffic is not an ADP business integration. Static approved local assets are distinct from business calls.

The source X-Ray iframe and its URL are excluded. X-Ray and Newsfeed may display only their admitted unavailable/empty product state, with no fictional provider results or publications. Other tabs/forms retain their approved product structure and local navigation, rather than collapsing ADP to one questionnaire.

The existing AppShell assistant remains the only shared assistant. Its current presentation controls and persisted profile/avatar continue to work; the ADP-specific assistant, conversation fixtures and screen-context code are excluded. No new Dock, personalization, module activation or parallel assistant is included. Benchmark Finder and X-Ray entry cards remain inactive.

## 7. Compatibility, future integration and withdrawal

This amendment does not activate real SRM business features. Future persistence, provider calls, organization-private data, signatures, mail, evaluations, Screen Context or CIC require separate owner contracts, E1 operation adoption and security/release evidence. They must consume the presentation contract rather than silently replacing it with private cross-app imports.

Compatible optional presentation fields or corrected labels/styles use normal versioned owner changes and consumer acceptance. Changes to required fields/events, lifecycle, ownership, identity/access, data scope or network/persistence capabilities require an explicit new contract/amendment and impact analysis. New real APIs cannot appear under the same presentation-only version.

Withdrawal is a controlled SG2 source release disabling the ADP entry/removing its mount, or rollback to the recorded previous SG2 artifact. It does not delete supplier data (none is created by this slice), reassign ownership or require an SRM runtime release. Existing routing/history is not erased.
