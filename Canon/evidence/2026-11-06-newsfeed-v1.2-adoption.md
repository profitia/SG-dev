## Precedence and boundaries

The amendment narrowly replaces the historical v1 exclusion of sharing and owner-only reads with owner-controlled individual/same-organisation read ACLs. It retains immutable creator and owner UserIDs, verified application/organisation/E1 context and private recipient state. Historical Newsfeed v1.0/v1.1 and Hub v1.1 remain ACTIVE and byte-identical; no historical evidence or fingerprint is rewritten. The new manifest entry has load order476, after its three existing active dependencies and before unrelated report/calendar entries. Existing entries are unchanged.

Saved definitions, versioned executions and publication/result relationships form three distinct persistent business layers. Dates and criteria snapshots remain distinct. Minimal Newsfeed metadata/link records have no default TTL; full articles, raw provider responses and stored AI interpretations/summaries remain excluded. Source Hub retention0, six UNMETERED policies, existing source licences and MarketsOFF are untouched. The new business index rule requires a future explicit Newsfeed schema/write-contract change; it is not a workaround for current retention validation.

`Odtwórz Newsfeed` / `REPLAY_ARCHIVE` reads the existing Newsfeed archive only: no Source Hub, provider/OpenAI request or new run. `Sprawdź nowe artykuły` is a separate, explicitly authorized new search with a new run. Sharing, replay, retention and recipient-state requirements describe future implementation, not functionality deployed by this adoption.
