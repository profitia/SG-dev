## Owner direction

The owner requested the ECR canon be updated directly through GitHub, without handing this writing task to Codex. Prior business decisions in the same discussion explicitly establish:

- One canonical economic-event meaning shared across Benchmark Finder, calendar, assistant and market reports.
- Canonical semantic definitions and event relevance are universal SHARED_KNOWLEDGE, not keyed or owned by userId or organisationId.
- DIRECT, STRONG and CONTEXTUAL relevance is a versioned relationship between an event/publication and a market, commodity, category or stable benchmark, with evidence and explanation.
- Private favourites, watched events, saved filters and personal presentation choices remain separate under current E1.
- The shared semantic layer belongs to ECR product storage; Benchmark Finder retains ownership of its benchmark catalogue; SourceHub retains provider access and upstream normalisation.
- The owner dispenses with arbitrary Stage02 business selection: the available and authorised event catalogue should remain available, with filters/personalisation at consumer level.
- Stage04 may implement the semantic model and overlay contract; this writing task is a canon update only.
