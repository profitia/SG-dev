## Owner's commercial decision

Within Profitia's existing Macrobond licence, the owner confirms that ordinary licensed benchmark history and metadata downloads/refreshes incur **no incremental charge per request**. This is the owner's commercial declaration, not an independent audit of the supplier contract. A documented contrary supplier/contract condition must be reconciled explicitly; request count alone is not evidence of a charge.

1. Standard authenticated, authorised reads of licensed benchmarks need no separate owner approval per download. This includes repeated full-history retrieval for Forecast, backtesting, verification and development.
2. Do not create arbitrary business download caps, treat the former D0 four-call boundary as a permanent rule, or charge each Macrobond call as a Package & Limits unit without an actual commercial basis. The previous task-specific boundary is superseded by this decision.
3. Automatic refresh according to product needs and source publication frequency is commercially authorised. Scheduling, implementation, service identity and environment activation still use their existing governed paths; this document creates no scheduler.
4. Cache, deduplication, bounded concurrency, timeout and controlled retry policies serve efficiency, correctness and technical safety. Audit may count requests without turning them into paid usage or a customer limit.
5. Keep provider licence coverage, provider rate limits, technical Source admission/budgets, optimisation, customer entitlements and commercial metering distinct. A technical refusal must identify its technical cause and must not be reported as a missing per-read business approval or supplier charge.
6. Preserve authentication, current E1/service authorisation, data isolation, auditing, provider terms and all accepted technical safeguards. This business decision does not modify a live Source policy, renew a grant, raise a capacity or authorise privileged fallback.
7. Other benchmarks under the same valid licence may use this standing business decision. Other applications retain their ownership, accepted licence/application scope and separate adoption; no access to an unlicensed dataset or paid add-on is implied.
